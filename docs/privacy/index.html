Privacy Policy

This policy explains what SubScript processes, where that processing happens, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: October 15, 2026 · Effective: September 20, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy covers the SubScript desktop creator tool and this public website. It does not replace the privacy policy of a game, operating system, hosting provider, or social platform you use alongside SubScript.

2. Local-first processing

SubScript is designed as a local desktop workflow. When you select a recording, replay-buffer clip, logo, music file, or other source, the app processes it on your computer using the features you choose. The public website is static and does not require you to upload gameplay files to read it.

Local processing can still create local copies, temporary files, rendered previews, logs, configuration files, and export folders. These may include personal information visible in your recordings or metadata. Review your file system and backup settings to understand where your computer stores them.

3. Files and content you provide

Depending on how you use the app, SubScript may access:

  • gameplay recordings, replay-buffer clips, and selected time ranges;
  • logos, intro and outro clips, music, caption inputs, and brand settings;
  • generated video, captions, metadata, manifests, and platform preparation files; and
  • technical configuration, paths, preferences, and local logs needed to operate the workflow.

SubScript uses these materials to perform the actions you request. You choose which files to select and can remove local files through your operating system or app workflow.

4. TikTok, YouTube, and other connected accounts

SubScript is operated by Branden Nail (Pelham, Alabama, USA). Connecting a social account is optional. Upload packs for TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, and Rumble work without any account connection.

TikTok

If you choose Connect TikTok, SubScript opens TikTok's official Login Kit authorization page in your browser and requests only these scopes:

  • user.info.basic: basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) that TikTok provides with sign-in. SubScript does not use it for anything beyond identifying the connected account;
  • video.upload: to send a video you approved to your TikTok inbox as a draft for you to finish in the TikTok app; and
  • video.publish: to post a video you approved directly to your TikTok profile, with the caption and privacy setting you choose. To do this, SubScript also asks TikTok which privacy options your account allows.

SubScript uploads only the specific clips you approve, one at a time, after you click to publish. It does not read your existing TikTok videos, followers, messages, comments, or analytics, and it does not post without your action.

YouTube

If you connect YouTube, SubScript uses Google's OAuth with the youtube.upload scope only, to upload clips you approve to your channel. SubScript's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Optional trend suggestions

If you turn on trend suggestions, SubScript sends the game name you set (for example, “Warzone”) and a region code to the YouTube Data API using your own API key, to find hashtags and keywords used in popular public gaming videos this week. No video, account information, or personal data is sent, and results are cached on your computer for 24 hours. This feature is off by default. SubScript does not scrape TikTok or Google Trends.

Where tokens are stored

Access and refresh tokens are stored only on your own computer, in the SubScript folder, encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI) so only your Windows user account can read them. They are never sent to us, to this website, or to any server other than the platform that issued them. We do not operate a server that receives your platform data or videos.

Disconnecting and revoking access

You can disconnect at any time with the Disconnect button next to TikTok or YouTube in SubScript’s Publishing settings, which deletes the saved sign-in from your computer. (Deleting the saved connection files in the SubScript folder has the same effect.) You can also revoke access from the platform: on TikTok, go to Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions (or Manage app permissions) and remove SubScript; on Google, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Data deletion

Because platform data stays on your computer, uninstalling SubScript and deleting its folder, removes it. Videos you already posted stay on the platform until you delete them there. For any deletion request or question, email sensoredrooster.com@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

5. Website data

This site consists of static files hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard technical information such as an IP address, request metadata, and security logs when serving the site. See GitHub’s own policies for its processing. SubScript does not intentionally add analytics, advertising trackers, contact forms, or third-party marketing scripts to these pages.

6. Third parties

SubScript may work with local tools such as FFmpeg and optional caption or OCR components, as well as third-party platforms you choose to connect or use. Those tools and platforms may process data according to their own terms and privacy policies. SubScript does not sell your content or personal information.

7. Retention and deletion

Because the main workflow is local, you generally control retention by deleting source files, temporary files, outputs, logs, configuration, and credential files from your computer and backups. Some third-party platforms may retain content or account data under their own policies. Website hosting logs may be retained by GitHub according to its policies.

If you ask us to delete information held by us, contact sensoredrooster.com@gmail.com with enough detail to identify the request. We may need to retain limited information where required by law, to resolve disputes, or to protect security.

8. Security

We use a local-first design and avoid placing secrets in this public site. No software or transmission method is completely secure, so keep your operating system, dependencies, backups, and credential files protected. Do not send access tokens, refresh tokens, client secrets, or private recordings through an untrusted channel.

9. Your choices

You may choose which files to process, whether to enable optional captions, music, or integrations, whether to keep or delete generated outputs, and whether to connect a social account. You can also revoke third-party authorization and request information or deletion by contacting us.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app or website changes. The current version will be posted here with a new “Last updated” date. Questions or privacy requests should be sent to sensoredrooster.com@gmail.com.
