Made for repeatable output

One approved moment. Many useful files.

The desktop app keeps the source workflow practical, transparent, and ready for your publishing routine.

01 — STYLE

Make it yours

Save a logo, corner placement, captions, music bed, intro, and outro so each new clip starts on-brand.

02 — REVIEW

Keep the gate

Review the master, landscape preview, and vertical preview. Approve as-is, trim and preview again, or reject.

03 — EXPORT

Leave with a pack

Approved clips can include horizontal and vertical video, captions, metadata, and platform-specific preparation folders.

Social-ready, not social-dependent

Prepare once. Publish on your terms.

  • YouTube Shorts: optional upload when you configure the desktop app's YouTube connection.
  • TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, and Rumble: local export packs and posting checklists are prepared by default; SubScript does not promise direct publishing for these destinations.
  • Credentials stay yours: OAuth files and tokens belong in your local configuration and are never part of this public website.
