Automated workflow setup

Play normally. Press one trigger. Get a clip.

This setup connects your recorder to SubScript. We will not publish anything until you choose what should happen after each clip.

  1. PrepareSave one replay from OBS or your recorder.
  2. ConnectTell SubScript where replays go and choose a shortcut.
  3. DecideChoose clip length and review or automatic delivery.

Step 1 of 4Prepare one replay before continuing.

1 / Prepare

Save one replay first

In OBS, enable Replay Buffer in Settings → Output, assign Save Replay a shortcut in Settings → Hotkeys, start Replay Buffer, and save one test replay. Other recorders should use their save-clip control.

This setup processes saved footage. SubScript does not press your recorder's Save Replay key for you. Wait until the replay finishes writing before you continue.

Check this box after the replay is saved.

OBS replay setup guide ↗

2 / Connect

Point SubScript at the replay folder

Use the exact folder where your recorder saves replay videos. A dedicated folder prevents unrelated recordings from being picked up. We verify that the folder exists when you test or save.

Use a different shortcut from your recorder's Save Replay key. During play: save the replay, wait for it to finish, then press this SubScript shortcut.

Enter a replay folder and a shortcut such as ctrl+shift+c.

3 / Decide

Choose what each trigger should do

Review first is safest: the workflow makes clips automatically, but you approve each one before publishing. Automatic mode skips that check and only uses destinations you enabled in Publish.

Choose a clip length between 5 and 300 seconds.

4 / Test

Make a safe preview, then turn it on

The safe test uses the newest replay in your folder and never publishes. Watch it in Review. If it looks right, return here and choose Save & start automated workflow.

Confirm the trigger order before arming the workflow.
