Choose your destinations

Choose whether clips wait for review or publish automatically. Manual destinations always create upload packs.

Publishing mode

Automatic mode applies to new VOD and live-hotkey clips. It saves your export pack and immediately sends clips to enabled destinations using their saved visibility. YouTube requires a working connection; other destinations currently create manual upload packs. Existing review clips remain pending.

Create titles, descriptions & tags

Supports YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X and Rumble. Turn on destinations below, enter your game and creator name, then generate sample drafts. For an actual clip, use Generate post drafts on its Review card.

Samples use local templates and the information above. They do not save settings, change a clip, render video, or publish anything. Clip drafts also use real captions when available.

Edit each platform draft in Review before approving. Automatic publishing uses the generated drafts immediately. When this is off, YouTube uses its title template and other saved settings.
